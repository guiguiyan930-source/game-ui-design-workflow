# Shop page brief

- Platform: desktop, landscape 16:9, 1920×1080.
- Game: 2D western-cartoon survival roguelike.
- Theme: warm painted wood, parchment, worn metal, green enamel.
- Layout: top resource bar; left category tabs; center 3×2 product grid; right adventurer preview; bottom upgrade progress.
- Primary action: purchase from the selected product card.
- States: default, loading, insufficient currency, sold out, error.
- Text strategy: page labels may be composited; component sprites must remain text-free.
